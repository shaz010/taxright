If it's free, what's the catch?
Who paid for this — and why?

It's a fair question. Most "free" things are free because you're the product — your data gets sold, your attention gets monetised, or you hit a paywall the moment you actually need something.

TaxRight is different for one simple reason: there is nothing to charge for. It's a single file that runs entirely inside your browser. There's no server processing your return, no database storing your information, no company with investors expecting a return.

Your tax data is never transmitted anywhere. It can't be — the app has no backend to send it to. It lives only in your browser, on your device, until you close the tab.

Think of it like a calculator someone built and left on a table. The calculator doesn't charge you per calculation. It doesn't know what numbers you typed. TaxRight is the same thing — just more sophisticated, and for taxes.
