## [1.0.0] — 2024-07-28

### Added
- Full US federal tax preparation for tax year 2024
- Support for W-2 employees, freelancers/self-employed, and small business owners
- 2024 IRS tax brackets for all 5 filing statuses (Single, MFJ, MFS, HOH, QSS)
- Schedule C net profit calculation (revenue, COGS, expenses)
- Self-employment tax (15.3%) with half-SE deduction
- QBI deduction §199A with income phase-outs
- Home office deduction — simplified and actual method
- Vehicle deduction — standard mileage (67¢/mile) and actual expenses
- Standard vs. itemized deduction optimizer
- Child Tax Credit & Additional CTC
- Child & Dependent Care Credit
- Education credits (American Opportunity & Lifetime Learning)
- Earned Income Tax Credit (EITC) using 2024 tables
- Retirement Saver's Credit
- Long-term capital gains with 2024 thresholds
- 1099-K classification wizard
- Audit risk scoring with specific IRS trigger flags
- Live animated refund/owed counter
- Chart.js waterfall chart and tax bracket position chart
- Quarterly estimated tax planner with 2025 due dates
- JSON export/import for session persistence
- Dark mode support
- Print-ready styles
- Zero backend — all calculations run in-browser, no data leaves the device
